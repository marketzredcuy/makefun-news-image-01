# news-image-01 — v2 newspaper

One image summing up the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026:
a newspaper front page with 30 stories — 8 illustrated main stories
(FAA halts all US departures, Iran says it hit a US radar in Bahrain,
Trump, Xi and Putin to meet in China, SpaceX Starship Flight 15 reaches
orbit, Ukraine's 1,200-drone attack, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns,
Microsoft 365 outage day 2, Pope Leo XIV says Mass in Chiclayo, Peru)
plus 22 more stories in the "ALSO ON FRIDAY" list. All facts verified
against real reporting of the day.

`main.py` copies the pre-composed `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` and verifies size and format.
