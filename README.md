# news-image-01 — v2 newspaper

One image summing up the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026:
a newspaper front page with 30 stories — a HERO lead story (FAA halts
all US departures) plus 10 more illustrated stories (Iran radar in
Bahrain, Trump-Xi-Putin APEC, SpaceX Starship Flight 15, Ukraine's
1,200-drone attack, Nvidia CEO resigns, Microsoft 365 outage day 2,
Pope Leo XIV in Peru, tanker hits cruise ship in New Orleans, Russian
drone sets a Kyiv hospital ablaze, EU picks five nations for Ukraine
peace talks) plus 19 more stories in the "ALSO ON FRIDAY" list. All
facts verified against real reporting of the day.

`main.py` copies the pre-composed `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` and verifies size and format.
